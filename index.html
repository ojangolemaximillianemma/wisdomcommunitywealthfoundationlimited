Who We Are

Wisdom begins when we choose to care.

Wisdom Foundation is a community-focused charitable organization dedicated to creating meaningful and sustainable change.

We believe that lasting development happens when communities are given the knowledge, resources and opportunities they need to build their own future.

  • Supporting access to quality education.
  • Empowering families and young people.
  • Supporting healthier communities.
  • Creating sustainable local opportunities.
